Which picture makes the banner

Key art first, by IGDB’s artwork type: key art without a logo, then key art with a logo, then an artwork, then concept art, landscape only. Never a logo, an icon, a cover or an infographic. Then the first screenshot. Then the cover, blurred. With no cover either, there is no banner.

The Witcher 3: key art without a logo. Its first artwork is a 128×128 icon, which the rule skips.
Disco Elysium: key art without a logo. Its first artwork is a transparent logo, which the rule skips.
Final Fantasy VII (1997): no art and no screenshots, so the blurred cover.
S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2: key art without a logo.
