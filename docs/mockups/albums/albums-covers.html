Album covers, the chips and the icon

Square covers

Every album cover is square, in a row, a grid, a tile’s fan and on the page, never cropped to 2:3. На межі (250×227) and The Road (250×224) aren’t square on the Cover Art Archive; they fill the square, losing a few pixels.

No cover: the typeset square

When the Cover Art Archive answers 404. As a book’s typeset cover (books/), square: the title at the top, the artist at the foot, a thin frame. At 56×56 (a web Search row) the title alone.
